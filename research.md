## 1. LEAD — Iran widens Gulf attacks (Middle East)

- **What happened (18 Nov):** Early Wednesday Iran's army said it targeted Ali Al Salem
  Air Base in Kuwait (hosts US forces) with drones; Kuwait's General Staff said the blasts
  heard came from air defences intercepting incoming attacks. Bahrain (home of the US
  5th Fleet) sounded sirens and said it intercepted "a number of Iranian aerial attacks".
- **Trigger (17 Nov):** a US fighter jet struck a Revolutionary Guard missile launch site
  on Qeshm Island in the Strait of Hormuz on Tuesday; that strike followed an Iranian
  attack on a US radar installation in Bahrain earlier on Tuesday.
- **Context:** first reported Iranian attack on Kuwait since September; the war began
  with US–Israeli strikes on Iran on 28 Feb 2026; AP analysis: Ali Al Salem struck at
  least 30 times by Iran during the war.
- **Sources:** AP via PBS NewsHour, "Iran widens Gulf attacks by targeting U.S. air base in
  Kuwait and sites in Bahrain" (18 Nov 2026,
  https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/iran-widens-gulf-attacks-by-targeting-u-s-air-base-in-kuwait-and-sites-in-bahrain);
  NBC News (https://www.nbcnews.com/world/iran/iran-widens-gulf-attacks-targeting-us-air-base-kuwait-sites-bahrain-rcna610128);
  CNN (https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/17/politics/us-iran-self-defense-strikes);
  The Globe and Mail; Just Security Early Edition 18 Nov (citing WSJ, Reuters).
- **Why it matters:** escalation of the Iran war onto a second Gulf state hosting US
  forces, on the day leaders gather at APEC.

## 3. Voyager 1 reaches one light-day from Earth (Space)

- **What happened (18 Nov, 10:16:07 UTC):** NASA's Voyager 1, launched in 1977, became the
  first human-made object one light-day from Earth (~25.9 billion km / 16.1 billion miles,
  173.14 AU): its radio signals take 24 hours to reach Earth.
- **Sources:** EarthSky, "Celebrate as Voyager 1 reaches 1 light-day from Earth"
  (https://earthsky.org/space/voyager-1-1-light-day-from-earth-november-17-18-2026/,
  citing NASA); AP (Marcia Dunn), 14–15 Nov 2026, milestone "on Wednesday".
